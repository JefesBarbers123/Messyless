02 / THE IDEA

WHY
MESSYLESS?

A burger shouldn't make you choose between getting stuck in and keeping your clothes clean.

01

SEAL IT.

The burger is built inside a pizza-style dough round and sealed with a 12cm cutter.

02

KEEP THE GOOD STUFF IN.

Two sauces are set as pucks and sit inside the build, protected by cheese layers.

03

FRY IT CRISPY.

Par-fried ahead of service, then finished to order for a deep-golden, crackly outside.

04

EVERY BITE COUNTS.

The result is designed around the mouthful: burger, cheese, sauce and crunch together instead of falling down your hands.
